Check-in and replan loop

How Unstuck stays current after the first plan: email check-in, student-reported progress, then adaptive replan when needed. Not the same as “next step only” (continuation) or “something changed” (plan update on screen 13).

What this loop is not

Different pathHow it differs
Plan continuation Dashboard “I am on track” → one next action, not full replan
Plan update Screen 13: deadline changed, new item, etc. — student-driven change, not check-in-driven
First session Full intake from empty state; master prompt, not replan

Narrative

The check-in loop is the accountability layer. Unstuck does not spy on Khan Academy or homework apps; it asks Matt what happened and adjusts the plan when he reports he is behind. That keeps trust and fits FERPA-minimal data collection.
